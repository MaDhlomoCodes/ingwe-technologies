About Us

Ingwe Technologies

Ingwe Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a privately registered South African company founded in March 2025 by Njabulo Mbatha. Built on the principles of stealth, strength, and agility, the company was established to deliver targeted solutions to the operational challenges faced by businesses across multiple industries.

Operating at the intersection of advanced drone applications, technology-driven solutions, and equipment sourcing and hire, Ingwe Technologies combines proven technical expertise with a client-centric delivery model.

Every engagement is tailored to the precise requirements and standards of the client — from initial scoping through to delivery, reporting, and follow-up support.

"We were built to solve problems that other companies couldn't. That's what Ingwe means to us."
Njabulo Mbatha — Founder, Ingwe Technologies

Drone Services
